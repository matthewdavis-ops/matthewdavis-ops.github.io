Clinical, Regulatory & Brand

Design

Most of the work on this page started with the same question: how do you take something complicated and make it usable by everyone from a hospital CEO to a CNA cleaning treatment rooms?

Some of these pieces translate regulatory requirements into decision-support tools. Others help partner facilities educate patients, train staff, or communicate with referral sources. Different projects, same goal: give someone the information they need in a form they can actually use.

Point-of-Decision Tools

Each one takes a rule — a federal coverage criterion, a pressure-vessel standard, a proposed payment model — and makes it actionable at the moment someone has to act on it.

Partner-Enablement Systems

Built to be used under somebody else’s name. Designed so a partner facility could deploy them immediately under its own brand. The goal isn’t recognition. It’s creating materials that work without a designer, a marketer, or a corporate office standing behind them.

Outreach

A series of business-development and brand narratives designed to answer the questions hospital and physician-practice leaders ask before entering a wound-care partnership. Different topics, same goal: reduce uncertainty and help decision-makers evaluate the offering clearly.

Recruitment

A recruitment campaign built to introduce students considering the field to wound care and hyperbaric medicine. Designed for large-format display at career fairs, campus events and internship outreach — qualifications, expectations and opportunities, readable quickly in a crowded room.

Partner Facility Illustrations

Custom architectural illustrations created for hospitals and physician practices across the country. Used in planning documents, executive presentations, business reviews and outreach materials to give each partner a recognizable visual presence within the network.
