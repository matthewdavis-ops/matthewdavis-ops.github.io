DatMavis.com · 2010 — 2015

DatMavis.com

My personal universe…er, website of more than 2,000 pages, every one designed in Photoshop and hand-coded in Dreamweaver, built as explorable worlds and stories rather than a listing of posts. And it’s live again, right here. A selection of the original pages, reassembled one HTML file and GIF at a time.

A few things before you go in. DatMavis.com is a work of fan fiction, built from parts of worlds and universes borrowed with love from the stories I grew up on. It’s not for sale and never will be: no ads, no merch shop, no tip jar. Free admission since 2010. And it’s still under RE-construction, so a few doors don’t open yet. When you find one, it isn’t a dead end. It’s just a room I haven’t finished restoring to its original glory.

The rest? Is…probably best discovered rather than explained.

There are planets you can visit. Stories hidden inside stories. Easter eggs galore. Places that only make sense after you’ve wandered through three others first. References borrowed from books, television, films, poetry, and whatever happened to be living rent-free in my noggin when the page was built.

The rabbit? Knows the way. Good luck, space cadet!
