Staff Newsletter · 2020 — Present

Essays

This started in 2020. COVID had arrived, people were struggling, and the answer was a weekly newsletter — a letter, a story, a piece of history, whatever the week needed — written, designed and sent out to keep heads above water. It ran that way for four years before the rest of the corporate team came in to share the load. The ten below are what the writing became.

They are not about wound care. They are about a fourteen-year-old in Idaho who looked at a plowed field and worked out television, a diner that got the coffee order wrong on purpose for three months, and a surgeon at Borodino who realized the order you treat people in is the one variable on that field you actually control.
