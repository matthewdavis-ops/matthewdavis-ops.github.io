Web Design

I’ve been designing and building websites since the late 1990s. For employers, clients, a podcast, bands, a business my wife and I owned, and some simply because I wanted to create something unconventionally cool. Technological advancements have, of course, transformed the discipline. My goals didn’t change: create an experience that helps people find what they’re looking for, lets them have some fun, and gives them a reason to stay.
