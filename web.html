Hand-Built Sites · 2011 — 2020

Web Design

Five sites, built across nine years for a band, a novelist, a furniture business and nobody in particular. None of them was anyone's job. There is no framework here, no CMS, no template and no stylesheet worth the name — these are HTML tables, images cut by hand in Photoshop, and a rollover state drawn for every single one of them.

Every screen below is a screenshot of the original files, opened in a current browser. Nothing was rebuilt, repaired or re-cut to produce them.

Before It Was Anyone's Job

In order. The useful thing about the order is that the idea arrives four years before the site it's famous for — the explorable room shows up in 2011, on a band's website, and DatMavis is the full-scale version of something already shipped.

103 images across these four pages, and every one of them has a second state — a hover variant, drawn separately and preloaded in JavaScript before the page finishes opening. The count of actual drawn artwork is therefore roughly double what any screenshot of it can show.
