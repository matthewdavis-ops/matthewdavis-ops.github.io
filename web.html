Five Sites · 2010 — Present

Web Design

Five sites across fifteen years, ten screens, one page each. One is the day job. The other four were built off the clock — for a band, a novelist, a furniture business and nobody in particular, with no framework, no platform, no template and no stylesheet worth the name: HTML tables, images cut by hand in Photoshop, and a rollover state drawn for every single one of them.

Every screen on these pages is a screenshot, not a mockup or a rebuild — the live site as it serves today, and the rest opened from their original files in a current browser.
