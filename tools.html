Shared Health Services · 2025 — 2026

Tools

These tools were built to solve problems I kept running into while supporting wound care and hyperbaric programs across the country. Every document drops into any partner site without editing, and every one is sourced to the rule, the regulation, or the clinical literature it rests on.

Rather than restating the requirements, most of these tools identify what is still missing from the record and what has to happen next to move a patient, a program, or a decision forward.

Every one is a working document, built for a real program and used in the field.

HBOT Screening System All fifteen of Medicare’s covered hyperbaric indications, organized by where screening a patient gets difficult: the rare emergencies on one call-now sheet, look-alike conditions separated into distinct pathways, broad criteria translated into the evidence each one actually requires, and diabetic wounds moved through their 30-day screening and reevaluation clock.
Coverage & Documentation Tools that translate payer policy, regulatory requirements and clinical evidence into practical documentation standards. Designed to identify what a patient’s record needs to contain, what’s still missing, and what must be established before a treatment proceeds or a claim is submitted, held to the requirements Medicare contractors, commercial payers, auditors and regulators actually apply.
Downtime Documentation Electronic health records can fail system-wide at any time. Patient care shouldn’t. This set is the paper record for that day: forms mirroring each EMR template field for field, a charge ticket for every encounter and a reconciliation log that doesn’t close until every entry, order and charge is back in the system.
Finance & Strategy Resources that turn reimbursement, staffing, operational and market data into financial models and decision-support tools for healthcare leaders evaluating new programs, service lines and care models. Built to forecast performance, compare alternatives, identify risk and support investment decisions before resources are committed.
Operations & Safety The systems a wound care or hyperbaric oxygen therapy center relies on to manage its daily operations. Tools that standardize patient intake, clinical documentation, communications, safety standards, staffing rhythms and patient flow so the work remains consistent across volumes, shifts, teams, staffing changes and locations.
Patient & Clinician Education Resources that put clinical evidence, referral pathways, treatment requirements and specialty knowledge into language patients, caregivers and referring providers can understand and act on, while preserving the accuracy required for medical decision-making.
